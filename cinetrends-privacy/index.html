CineTrends Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CineTrends handles information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

CineTrends does not require account creation and does not collect personal profile information such as your name or phone number inside the app.

The app stores selected preferences locally on your device, such as:

  • Theme preference (system/light/dark)
  • Your watchlist items
  • Your selected TV app filters

How Data Is Used

Local app data is used only to provide app functionality (for example, showing your saved watchlist and settings). This local data is not used to identify you personally.

Third-Party Services

CineTrends displays movie information and images powered by TMDB.

Children's Privacy

CineTrends is not directed to children under 13 and does not allow account creation or direct submission of personal information. If you believe personal information from a child under 13 was shared with us, contact technymedia1@gmail.com and we will promptly review and address the request.

Changes To This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact
